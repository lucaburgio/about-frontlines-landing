Choose your war

Three ways to win

Every mode is played on the same territory board — but the objective, and the way you have to fight for it, changes completely.

01 — Domination

Domination

Conquer the island. Eliminate every enemy unit before they eliminate yours — no control points, no shortcuts.

02 — Conquest

Conquest

Hold the control points and grind down enemy points with every unit you kill. First side to zero loses.

03 — Breakthrough

Breakthrough

Attack or defend across a chain of sectors. The attacker must seize every control point — the defender deploys right on the front line.
